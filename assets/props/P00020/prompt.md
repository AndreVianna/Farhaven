# Shelf Fungus (P00020) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the style anchor for the entire
Farhaven prop set. Keep art style, lighting, background, and composition
identical — only the silhouette and biology change per species.

## Species identity

Alien shelf fungus (bracket fungus) that only grows on **fallen rotting
wood**. Each variant depicts the same species of fungus growing on the
trunk of a different fallen Farhaven tree, narratively marking where
that species ended its life. The shelf-fungus caps themselves read
consistently across variants — fan-shaped brackets fanning horizontally
out of the host trunk's bark, stacked in natural tiers.

**FIXED (shelf fungus identity):**
- Cap color (warm cream-white upper surface with bands of dusty orange-rust
  and faint dark olive edges; lower surface is a denser off-white pore
  layer; faint cyan bioluminescent edging along the outer rim)
- Cap shape (fan / semicircle bracket ~10-20 cm wide, 1-2 cm thick at
  the trunk-attached edge tapering to a thin outer rim; stacked in
  overlapping tiers of 2-4 brackets per cluster)
- Growth attachment (fungi emerge horizontally from the side of the
  fallen host trunk; no stalk — attached directly to the wood)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single host trunk segment + fungi, 3/4 high-angle,
  #888 gray background, soft studio lighting)

**VARIES (three host-tree scenes — each tells which species died here):**
- **v1 — Sap Fruit Tree trunk host:** warm gray-brown bark with faint
  amber-orange veins (matches P00013)
- **v2 — Wideleaf Tree trunk host:** warm gray-brown bark with subtle
  vertical texture, thicker more gnarled segment (matches P00010)
- **v3 — Stalk Plant trunk host:** cream-to-tan stalk with pronounced
  darker concentric banding rings (matches P00005) — distinctly NOT
  traditional bark
