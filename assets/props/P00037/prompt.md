# Glow Cap (P00037) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the style anchor. Keep art
style, lighting, background, and composition identical — only the
silhouette and biology change per species.

## Species identity

**The signature Forest fungus.** A cluster of classic cap-and-stipe
mushrooms with dramatic **bright cyan bioluminescent caps** that glow
vividly enough to light the forest floor around them. The mushroom
that marks "this is Forest" visually.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (caps are the signature feature: vivid cyan-blue
  bioluminescent upper surface with a pearl-white inner ring near the
  edge and a darker cobalt-blue center; gills underneath are pale
  luminous mint that glow too; slender ivory-white stipe with faint
  blue ring; ground base shows slight dark loamy moisture)
- Material (smooth rounded caps with a visible soft glow, thin gills
  underneath, smooth waxy stipe)
- Overall growth form (**a cluster of several individual mushrooms
  emerging from a shared ground patch** — caps at varying heights and
  sizes, all glowing cyan)
- Scale (cluster 30 cm wide, 3-5 mushrooms per cluster, tallest
  mushroom 18 cm with cap 8 cm across)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single patch, 3/4 high-angle, gray background, soft
  studio lighting; glow must be clearly visible)

**VARIES (three maturation-stage variants):**
- **v1 — button stage:** young small closed caps, lower glow intensity
- **v2 — open mature:** fully expanded caps with visible gills, peak glow
- **v3 — aged fading:** larger flatter caps starting to warp, softer
  more diffuse glow
