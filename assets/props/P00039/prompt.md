# Coral Fungus (P00039) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the style anchor. Keep art
style, lighting, background, and composition identical — only the
silhouette and biology change per species.

## Species identity

A **branching coral-like fungus** (inspired by Ramaria/Clavaria) — no
cap or stipe, just dense upright finger-like branches reaching
skyward from a compact base, like orange-pink underwater coral on
land. Visually surprising — looks more like a reef creature than a
mushroom.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (vibrant coral-orange at the lower main branches
  transitioning to soft cream-pink at the branch tips, as if dipped
  in cream; small cyan bioluminescent glint dots scattered across a
  few branch tips)
- Material (dry chalky texture, fibrous feel, thickening toward the
  trunk-base and thinning dramatically toward branch tips)
- Overall growth form (**dense tight upright cluster of finger-like
  branches fanning out from a compact central trunk, forking
  repeatedly as they rise; no cap, no stipe — entirely branching**)
- Scale (15-20 cm tall, cluster spread 15 cm at the widest branching,
  base trunk 3 cm thick)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject, 3/4 high-angle, gray background, soft
  studio lighting)

**VARIES (three growth-density variants):**
- **v1 — small compact:** 20 branches, tight low cluster
- **v2 — medium branched:** 40 branches, taller with more splits
- **v3 — dense tall:** 60+ branches, tallest with many forks, most
  cyan glints
