## Species identity

Small alien wildflower with **yellow daisy-style blooms** that face upward.
Each flower is a flat radial head: about fourteen narrow pointed petals
arranged around a rust-brown central disc. Multiple individual stems rise
separately from the ground (NOT from a shared bulb or rosette), scattered
over a small patch.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (saturated sunny-yellow petals, rust-brown central disc,
  desaturated teal-green leaves, slender cream-green stems)
- Flower shape (flat radial daisy head, ~14 narrow pointed petals
  arranged around a rounded central disc ~1 cm across; flower face ~4 cm
  across; petals held upright-facing)
- Stem shape (straight, slender, 20-25 cm tall, firm upright — does
  not droop at the tip)
- Leaf shape (basal rosette of narrow spoon-shaped teal-green leaves at
  the ground level; a few small leaves on the stem)
- Growth habit (**each stem emerges individually from the ground** —
  not from a shared bulb; the patch is a scatter of 4-10 separate stems
  over ~35 cm)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single patch, 3/4 high-angle, #888 gray background, soft
  studio lighting)

**VARIES (three reproductive cycle stages):**
- **v1 — mixed:** open daisy heads + some closed conical buds, normal density
- **v2 — budding only:** fewer stems, all tips bear closed conical yellow
  buds, no open flowers
- **v3 — full bloom:** more stems and denser patch, all tips bear open
  daisy heads, no buds
