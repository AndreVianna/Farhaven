A **giant tropical lily-style flower** inspired by Oriental Lily /
Stargazer — large 6-petaled bowl-shaped blooms 20 cm across on tall
elegant stems, rich magenta-pink with darker speckle patterns and
clearly glowing bioluminescent petal edges. Forest exotic: rare,
dramatic, and visibly radiant at night.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (saturated magenta-pink petals with darker wine-red
  speckle patterns running from center outward along the petal
  midline; bright cyan bioluminescent edge highlights on every petal
  margin, unmistakably glowing; golden-yellow stamen cluster in the
  bowl center; smooth pale green stem; long narrow lance-shaped
  teal-green leaves)
- Material (smooth waxy large petals, thin wiry stamens, smooth stem,
  slightly glossy leaves)
- Overall growth form (**a tall slender stem with a cluster of 2-4
  large bowl-shaped lily flowers at the top, plus a few leaves
  alternating up the length of the stem**)
- Scale (total plant 1.2 m tall, flower diameter 20 cm, leaves 18 cm long)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject framed vertically, 3/4 high-angle, gray
  background, soft studio lighting)

**VARIES (three reproductive cycle stages):**
- **v1 — flowering peak:** 3-4 open lilies in full bloom, maximum

- **v2 — budding:** flower cluster is closed tight buds only, subtle
  glow visible through the closed petal seams
- **v3 — single lingering bloom:** only one flower still open, 1-2
