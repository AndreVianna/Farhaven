# Field Mushroom (P00019) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the style anchor for the entire
Farhaven prop set. Keep art style, lighting, background, and composition
identical — only the silhouette and biology change per species.

## Species identity

Small alien field mushroom growing in clusters of a few stems per patch.
Classic cap-and-stipe mushroom silhouette — a rounded brown cap on top of
a slender cream stalk. The cap is a warm medium-brown matte surface with
distinctive scattered **deep purple blotches/spots** breaking the brown —
the purple is the species' visual signature. Underneath the cap, pale
cream gills radiate from the stalk.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (warm medium-brown cap with deep purple irregular blotches
  and spots scattered across the upper surface, pale cream gills beneath,
  cream-ivory stalk with a very slight pinkish cast at the base)
- Cap shape (rounded convex cap, ~6-8 cm across when mature)
- Stalk shape (slender cylindrical stalk ~1.5 cm thick, 8-10 cm tall,
  with a small thin ring/skirt partway up where the cap margin once
  attached)
- Growth habit (**a few individual mushroom stems emerging from the same
  ground patch**, scattered loosely rather than a tight cluster — 3-6
  mushrooms visible across the patch)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single patch, 3/4 high-angle, #888 gray background, soft
  studio lighting)

**VARIES (three maturation stages — fruiting body life cycle):**
- **v1 — button stage:** small tight closed cap sits low over the stalk,
  cap edge still hugging the stalk, no gills visible, few mushrooms
- **v2 — open mature:** caps fully expanded into rounded domes, gills
  clearly visible on the underside, peak specimens
- **v3 — aged:** caps starting to flatten and warp with deeper shadow
  crevices, purple blotches darker, a couple of caps showing small
  decay discoloration spots, reads as a patch near end of cycle
