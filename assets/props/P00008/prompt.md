# Tall Reed Grass (P00008) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

The v1 `reference.png` of P00001 (Blade Grass) is the style anchor for the
entire Farhaven prop set. When generating this prop through nano-banana,
keep the art style, lighting, background, and general composition identical —
only the silhouette and biology differ.

## What varies between variants / What stays fixed

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (pale jade-green stalks, soft tan/cream tips near the top, faint bioluminescent edges)
- Material (tall reed — hollow, upright, slightly translucent)
- Overall growth form (vertical cluster, sparse-to-dense standing stems)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject, 3/4 high-angle, gray background, studio lighting)
- "Alien planet flora" feel

**VARIES (individual biological variation):**
- Number of stems (few tall vs many clustered)
- Stem lean (strictly vertical vs gently leaning outward)
- Tip state (fresh closed bud vs open feathery tip vs bent/aged)
- Overall proportions (tall-thin cluster vs wider base)
