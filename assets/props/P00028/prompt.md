# Leaf Carpet (P00028) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the style anchor. Keep art
style, lighting, background, and composition identical — only the
silhouette and biology change per species.

## Species identity

**Fallen-leaf litter** covering patches of the Forest floor. Not a living
plant — a composition of many large dropped leaves layered flat or
curled, reading as recently-fallen foliage from the canopy above. This
is what replaces grass in the Forest biome. Flat prop — almost no
height, spreads horizontally.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (rich leaf-litter mix: amber-browns, warm rust-oranges,
  olive-yellow edges, deep maroon-on-burgundy in the deepest layers;
  subtle hints of teal-green on recently-fallen leaves still holding
  some life; very faint cyan bioluminescent glow along the leaf veins
  of the freshest leaves)
- Material (broad flat leaves with visible veins, dry papery texture
  on the older layer, slightly softer on freshest leaves)
- Overall growth form (**flat horizontal carpet of overlapping fallen
  leaves — no central plant, no stem, no vertical growth; spreads as
  a broad patch on the ground**)
- Leaf shapes: broad palmate / lobed / heart-shaped leaves 15-30 cm
  long each, mixed orientations (some face-up, some flipped, some
  curled)
- Scale (50-70 cm wide patch, 2-4 cm thick layer, essentially flat)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single patch, 3/4 high-angle, gray background, soft
  studio lighting)

**VARIES (three density/freshness variants):**
- **v1 — fresh layer:** mix of recently fallen leaves in brighter
  ambers/oranges with some teal hints, scattered not yet dense
- **v2 — thick bed:** dense multi-layer of mixed-age leaves, deeper
  warm palette, many overlapping
- **v3 — decaying edge:** older mostly-decomposed layer, muted burgundy
  and dark olive, small fungal hints emerging through the gaps
