## Species identity

A **low fern with gigantic broad undivided fronds** — not pinnate like
Tree Fern, but huge single-blade fronds like banana leaves or giant
heart-shaped Monstera leaves. Sits directly on the forest floor with
no visible trunk — fronds radiate from a low compact base. Reads as
lush tropical undergrowth creating near-impassable density.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (rich deep forest-green on the upper frond surfaces
  with cool blue-green shadow tints in the veins; pale sage-green on
  the undersides; very faint cyan bioluminescent edge highlights at
  the frond tips)
- Material (thick waxy broad-blade fronds with visible deep vein
  structure fanning out from a central midrib; slightly glossy surface
  highlights)
- Overall growth form (**low base + huge undivided blade fronds
  radiating outward at different heights** — like a clump of giant
  banana leaves fanning from a ground-level base)
- Scale (crown 1.5-2 m wide, fronds 60-120 cm long each, tallest frond
  tip 1.2 m tall)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject, 3/4 high-angle, gray background, soft
  studio lighting)

**VARIES (three growth-stage variants):**
- **v1 — mature baseline:** 6 full-sized fronds, dense spread
- **v2 — young small:** 3 smaller fronds, compact
- **v3 — giant old:** 9 massive fronds, some with tears/weathering,
