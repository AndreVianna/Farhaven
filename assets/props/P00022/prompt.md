## Species identity

A Forest tree whose identity is its **dramatic buttress roots** — thick
fin-like root plates that rise up to half the height of the trunk before
merging into the main stem. Inspired by Amazonian kapok/ceiba trees.
Non-fruiting. Reads at a glance as "old, rooted, immovable."

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (warm gray-tan trunk darkening to deep olive-brown in the
  root flares; compact mid-height teal-green canopy with cooler shadow
  tints; subtle cyan bioluminescent seams along the root plate edges
  where moss has colonized)
- Material (smooth-rounded bark on trunk, slightly rougher on the root
  plates; painterly foliage masses)
- Overall growth form (**massive fin-like buttress roots radiating out
  from the base, rising 1.5–2 m up the trunk before merging** — roots
  thin and tall, trunk between them narrower and cylindrical above;
  crown sits mid-height, wider than tall)
- Scale (4–5 m tall, trunk 60 cm above buttress, root spread 2.5 m wide
  at ground)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject framed vertically, 3/4 high-angle, gray
  background, soft studio lighting)

**VARIES (three growth-stage variants):**
- **v1 — mature baseline:** 4.5 m, 4 prominent buttress roots
- **v2 — young:** 3 m, 2-3 subtle root flares just forming at the base
- **v3 — ancient:** 5 m with 6+ buttress roots forming a chambered base,
  stronger cyan glow along the root seams
