# Canopy Vine (P00027) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the style anchor. Keep art
style, lighting, background, and composition identical — only the
silhouette and biology change per species.

## Species identity

A standalone vine prop designed to read as "a cipó spanning between
neighboring trees." The mesh itself is self-contained (begins and ends
in the air) but its silhouette forms a clear **arch or U-curve** so
that when it is populated adjacent to Forest trees it visually bridges
them. No trees appear in the reference image — only the vine arc —
but the composition must make the bridging intent readable.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (deep olive-green ropey outer surface with subtle
  rust-brown highlights at knotted points; small widely-scattered
  teal-green leaf clusters along the vine; very faint cyan
  bioluminescent pulse inside the vine core, visible through slight
  translucence at the thinnest sections)
- Material (thick ropey woody vine with visible knotted twist, tough
  but flexible — reads like jungle liana)
- Overall growth form (**arched / U-inverted strand of woody vine
  spanning horizontally through open air, both ends extending
  upward-outward as if anchored to out-of-frame branches; sparse
  small leaf clusters along the length**)
- Scale (3.5 m span horizontally, 1.5 m tall at the arch peak, vine
  thickness 5 cm average)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject centered, 3/4 high-angle, gray background,
  soft studio lighting — no trees visible, just the floating vine arc)

**VARIES (three density variants — NOT reproductive cycle; this prop
scales visual density rather than phases):**
- **v1 — single arc:** one clean vine strand in a gentle arch
- **v2 — twin vines:** two parallel vine strands forming a rope-like
  pair, lightly entwined
- **v3 — tangled cluster:** three to four vines crossing each other in
  a messy woven cluster, denser mid-span
