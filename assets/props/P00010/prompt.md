# Wideleaf Tree (P00010) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

The v1 `reference.png` of P00001 (Blade Grass) is the style anchor for the
entire Farhaven prop set. Keep the art style, lighting, background, and
general composition identical — only the silhouette and biology differ.

## What varies between variants / What stays fixed

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (warm gray-brown bark, desaturated teal-green canopy with
  faint dusty blue-gray highlights in shadow, faint cyan bioluminescent
  edges on leaf tips)
- Material (sturdy woody trunk, matte painterly foliage in broad leaf
  clusters rather than individual leaves)
- Overall growth form (**bushy irregular spreading tree, English-oak
  style** — single sturdy trunk that branches low into several gnarled
  limbs radiating outward; broad rounded canopy wider than tall)
- Scale (small tree, ~2.5 m tall with a canopy ~3 m wide)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject, 3/4 high-angle, gray background, studio lighting)
- "Alien planet flora" feel

**VARIES (individual biological variation):**
- Canopy fullness (dense adult vs sparser young vs gnarled old)
- Trunk proportions (straight vs leaning vs twisted)
- Branch count and layout
