# Geode Half (P01009) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the **style anchor** for the
entire Farhaven prop set. Art style, lighting, background, and composition
must match. Only the silhouette/material changes per species.

Species identity: a **naturally broken alien geode** — an irregular
lumpy stone mass that has cracked open along a fault, revealing a
crystal-lined interior cavity through the crack opening. **CRITICAL:
this is NOT a cleanly sliced gift-shop geode** — the external surface
is irregular, chunky, natural; the opening is a jagged crack, not a
flat polished cut. **UNCOMMON rarity**. Yields raw_crystal + stone per
harvest.

## What varies / What stays fixed

**FIXED (material identity):**
- External form: an irregular chunky lumpy rock mass, asymmetric
  bulges and concavities, NOT round or ovoid or symmetric. It should
  read indistinguishable from a random chunk of natural stone from
  the outside
- Opening: a jagged irregular CRACK or BREAK running across one side
  of the rock — the crack has uneven edges, sharp fracture lines,
  maybe multiple intersecting break lines — NOT a clean flat slice
  revealing a perfect circle of interior
- Through the crack: the hollow interior cavity is visible, lined
  with tightly packed small crystal prisms (druse) growing inward
- Outer shell: rough matte stone, dull gray-brown, weathered,
  heavily textured with pits and irregularities
- Scale (22-30 cm across)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte painterly)
- Composition (single subject, 3/4 high-angle, #888 gray background,
  soft even studio lighting with subtle rim light)

**VARIES (interior crystal color per variant):**
- V1: **amethyst/violet interior** (classic alien geode)
- V2: **pale green interior** (rarer, more alien)
- V3: **smoky quartz interior, near-clear with dark hints** (most subtle)
