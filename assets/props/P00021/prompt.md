# Canopy Giant (P00021) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the style anchor for the entire
Farhaven prop set. Keep art style, lighting, background, and composition
identical — only the silhouette and biology change per species.

## Species identity

The dominant Forest canopy tree. A tall, straight-trunked giant that forms
the upper layer of the dense alien jungle — imposing silhouette, high crown,
lots of biomass between player height and crown. Non-fruiting. Its function
in the scene is structural: it defines the vertical limit of the Forest and
the shade below.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (warm gray-brown trunk with faint amber-copper undertones
  in the bark crevices, dark teal-green canopy with cooler blue-green
  shadow tints, faint cyan bioluminescent speckles scattered through the
  foliage on the underside of the canopy)
- Material (straight rigid woody trunk with smooth vertical bark striations,
  dense painterly foliage masses)
- Overall growth form (**single very tall straight trunk rising to a
  high dense canopy** — trunk takes up the lower 70% of the silhouette,
  crown occupies the top 30% as a broad rounded mass)
- Scale (5–6 m tall specimen with a canopy 3–4 m across at the top,
  trunk 30–40 cm thick)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject framed vertically, 3/4 high-angle, gray
  background, soft studio lighting)
- "Alien rainforest giant" feel

**VARIES (three growth-stage variants — NOT reproductive cycle since
this species is non-fruiting):**
- **v1 — mature baseline:** 5 m tall, full dense canopy, uniform straight trunk
- **v2 — young slim:** 3.5 m tall, narrower trunk, smaller tighter canopy
- **v3 — elder giant:** 6 m tall, thicker buttressed base, irregular crown
  with some dead branch stubs near the top, strongest bioluminescent
  speckle density
