# Cluster Berry (P00012) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

The v1 `reference.png` of P00001 (Blade Grass) is the style anchor for the
entire Farhaven prop set. Keep the art style, lighting, background, and
general composition identical — only the silhouette and biology differ.

## What varies between variants / What stays fixed

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (slender woody stems in warm gray-brown, small rounded
  leaves in deep muted teal-green; signature ripe feature: clustered
  **deep blue berries** rich indigo-to-cobalt)
- Material (compact bushy shrub, woody thin stems, rounded small leaves,
  glossy-matte berry skin)
- Overall growth form (small bushy shrub, multiple stems splaying outward
  from a low compact base, ~45 cm tall and ~55 cm wide)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject, 3/4 high-angle, gray background, studio lighting)
- "Alien planet flora" feel

**VARIES (three reproductive cycle stages — NOT size/age variants):**
- **v1 — flowering / unripe:** small white-pink flowers + small green immature berries
- **v2 — dormant:** no flowers, no berries, leaves only
- **v3 — ripe harvest:** dense clustered deep blue berries, no flowers

Same individual bush, three phases of its annual cycle.
