# Azure Bell (P00014) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the style anchor for the entire
Farhaven prop set. Keep art style, lighting, background, and composition
identical — only the silhouette and biology change per species.

## Species identity

Small alien wildflower with **bell-shaped deep-blue blooms** hanging off
slender drooping stem tips. Each flower is a fused tube with five pointed
lobes flaring at the mouth — like a foxglove or bluebell. Multiple
individual stems rise separately from the ground (NOT from a shared bulb),
scattered naturally over a small patch, giving the impression of wildflowers
growing where they landed.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (rich royal-blue flower tubes with slightly darker blue
  tips on the lobes; warm cream-yellow inner throat; pale green-cream
  stems in a matte satin finish; narrow alternating teal-green leaves)
- Flower shape (bell / tube, 5 flared lobes, ~2 cm long, ~1.5 cm across
  at mouth, drooping from stem tip)
- Stem shape (slender, 15-20 cm tall, gently arched at the top so the
  flower hangs pendulously)
- Leaf shape (narrow lance-shaped, alternating up the stem, 2-3 cm long)
- Growth habit (**each stem emerges individually from the ground** —
  not from a shared bulb; the patch is a scatter of 4-10 separate stems
  over ~35 cm)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single patch, 3/4 high-angle, #888 gray background, soft
  studio lighting)

**VARIES (three reproductive cycle stages):**
- **v1 — mixed:** open blue bells + a few closed spiral buds, normal density
- **v2 — budding only:** fewer stems, all tips bear closed tight buds,
  no open flowers
- **v3 — full bloom:** more stems and denser patch, all tips bear open
  flared bells, no buds
