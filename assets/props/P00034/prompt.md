## Species identity

A large exotic tropical flower inspired by **Heliconia / Red Ginger** —
vertical torch-like bract stacks on a tall stem with broad banana-style
leaves at the base. Big and bold, not a small wildflower. Signature:
stacked red bracts rising like a flame, with small bioluminescent true
flowers peeking from between them.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (saturated deep crimson-red bracts with slightly darker
  maroon shadow edges; inner true flowers pale cream with faint cyan
  bioluminescent tips peeking out between bract overlap; tall smooth
  stem in deep forest-green; broad banana-style leaves in dark
  teal-green with cooler blue-green shadow veins)
- Material (thick waxy bracts arranged in overlapping vertical stack,
  smooth cane-like stem, broad blade leaves)
- Overall growth form (**a tall upright stem rising from a low base
  of large banana-style leaves; at the stem top a vertical torch-like
  spike of stacked alternating red bracts 30-50 cm tall**)
- Scale (total plant height 1.5 m, torch spike 40 cm, leaves 60 cm long)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject framed vertically, 3/4 high-angle, gray
  background, soft studio lighting)

**VARIES (three reproductive cycle stages):**
- **v1 — flowering / unripe:** red bract torch full, inner cream
  flowers clearly peeking out between bracts with bioluminescent tips
- **v2 — post-bloom / spent:** bracts still present but browned and
  dried at tips, no cream flowers visible, reads as past-peak
- **v3 — new bloom peak:** fresh torch at maximum saturation, larger
  than v1, strongest bioluminescent peek from inner flowers
