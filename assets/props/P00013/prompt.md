# Sap Fruit Tree (P00013) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

The v1 `reference.png` of P00001 (Blade Grass) is the style anchor for the
entire Farhaven prop set. Keep the art style, lighting, background, and
general composition identical — only the silhouette and biology differ.

## What varies between variants / What stays fixed

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (warm gray-brown smooth bark with faint amber-orange
  veins, desaturated teal-green pendant-shaped leaves; ripe signature:
  large teardrop sap-fruits in rich warm amber-honey color with
  translucent wet-looking highlights and faint cyan bioluminescent edges)
- Material (smooth slightly glossy bark, matte leaves, translucent
  amber fruits that read as sap-filled pendant sacs in ripe form)
- Overall growth form (single straight trunk, narrow columnar canopy,
  a few main branches reaching upward-outward; ~3 m tall with a narrow
  ~1.8 m canopy)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject, 3/4 high-angle, gray background, studio lighting)
- "Alien planet flora" feel

**VARIES (three reproductive cycle stages — NOT size/age variants):**
- **v1 — flowering / unripe:** bright red flowers + small green immature fruits
- **v2 — dormant:** no flowers, no fruits, leaves only
- **v3 — ripe harvest:** large translucent amber sap-fruits, no flowers

Same individual tree, three phases of its annual cycle.
