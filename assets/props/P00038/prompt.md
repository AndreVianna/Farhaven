## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the style anchor. Keep art

## Species identity

An alien fungus inspired by **Phallus indusiatus (bamboo bride)** — a
tall slender stipe topped with a dark brown hooded cap, and most
distinctively a **delicate lacy white skirt-veil that hangs below the
cap** like a bride's net veil. One of the forest's most striking
fungi: elegant and slightly eerie.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (dark olive-to-chocolate brown honeycomb-textured
  bell-shaped cap; slender creamy-white spongy stipe; pristine
  lace-white indusium skirt-veil that drapes like a net curtain from
  beneath the cap down past mid-stipe; faint cyan bioluminescent
  glow on the inside of the skirt's lacy openings only)
- Material (spongy stipe texture, textured honeycomb cap, delicate
  lace-mesh skirt with tiny visible open cells)
- Overall growth form (**a single tall elegant mushroom per prop
  occasionally paired — stipe rising from a shared ground base, cap
  at the top, lace veil descending**)
- Scale (22-30 cm tall, skirt 12-15 cm long reaching past mid-stipe)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject, 3/4 high-angle, gray background, soft
  studio lighting)

**VARIES (three emergence-stage variants):**
- **v1 — emerging egg:** the veil-lady is still inside an egg-like
  white membrane on the ground, just starting to break open with the
  cap visible at the tip
- **v2 — mature open:** one fully-developed specimen with fully-draped
  skirt veil
- **v3 — paired mature:** two specimens from a shared base, both with
  full veils, slight height/rotation difference
