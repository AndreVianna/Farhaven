## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the style anchor. Keep art

## Species identity

A **tree-sized fern** — Dicksonia/Cyathea style — with a sturdy fibrous
trunk supporting a crown of enormous feathery fronds radiating outward
and arching down. The Forest's fern canopy sub-layer. Stands taller
than a person.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (dark chocolate-brown fibrous trunk with slightly
  reddish hairy coating, deep teal-green fronds with cooler blue-green
  shadow tints on the undersides, faint cyan bioluminescent veins
  along the central rachis of each frond)
- Material (trunk is covered in matted hair-like reddish-brown root
  fibers, fronds are finely pinnate painterly masses)
- Overall growth form (**single vertical fibrous trunk with a
  radiating crown of large arching fronds**; crown curves outward
  and slightly downward at frond tips like a fern umbrella)
- Scale (2–2.5 m tall, trunk 20-25 cm thick, crown spread 2.5-3 m)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject framed vertically, 3/4 high-angle, gray
  background, soft studio lighting)

**VARIES (three growth-stage variants):**
- **v1 — mature baseline:** 2 m tall, 7-8 fronds, full arching crown
- **v2 — young:** 1.2 m tall, slimmer trunk, 4 smaller fronds still unfurling
- **v3 — old tall:** 2.5 m tall, thicker fibrous trunk, 10+ fronds
  including some ragged older ones, most pronounced bioluminescence
