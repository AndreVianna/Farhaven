## Species identity

A tropical flower inspired by **Strelitzia / Bird of Paradise** — the
flower head has a dramatic bird-like silhouette with orange upward
"wings" and a deep purple arching "beak" structure. Sculptural,
architectural, and immediately recognizable. Forest exotic, rare.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (vivid orange upright "wing" petals with golden highlights;
  deep royal-purple curved "beak" arching horizontally; rich teal-green
  spathe (the boat-shaped structure from which the flower emerges)
  with darker shadow edges; smooth pale-green stem; broad paddle-shaped
  teal-green leaves; faint cyan bioluminescent highlights along the
  edges of the orange wings and at the tip of the purple beak)
- Material (firm waxy flower parts, architectural clean edges, smooth
  stem, broad waxy leaves)
- Overall growth form (**a tall stem with a single bird-like flower
  head at the top, rising from a basal cluster of long paddle-shaped
  leaves**)
- Scale (1.1-1.3 m tall, flower head 25 cm long, leaves 70 cm long)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject framed vertically, 3/4 high-angle, gray
  background, soft studio lighting)

**VARIES (three reproductive cycle stages):**
- **v1 — flowering peak:** one fully-open bird-silhouette flower head
- **v2 — emerging:** spathe still partly closed, only the tips of the
  orange wings peeking out, glow faint
- **v3 — multi-head crest:** two fully-open flowers at different angles
  on the same stem, strongest glow
