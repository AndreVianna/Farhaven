## Species identity

A Forest canopy tree whose signature is **long hanging bioluminescent
vines draped from its branches** — the vines are part of the same mesh
(the reproductive structure of this species, not separate plants).
Fruiting variant: the vines bear small glowing pod-flowers in cycle.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (warm gray-brown trunk, dark teal-green canopy with
  cooler blue-green shadow tints; signature **long pale cyan-to-jade
  bioluminescent vines** hanging 1–2 m down from the lower canopy
  branches — clearly brighter than the rest of the tree, distinctly
  glowing against the darker canopy)
- Material (rigid woody trunk with vertical striations, painterly
  canopy foliage, vines rendered as slender strand-like masses with
  subtle inner glow)
- Overall growth form (**single straight trunk + moderate rounded
  canopy + many hanging bioluminescent vines trailing downward**)
- Scale (5 m tall, trunk 30 cm thick, canopy 3 m across, vines hang
  1.5–2 m below canopy edge)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject framed vertically, 3/4 high-angle, gray
  background, soft studio lighting)

**VARIES (three reproductive cycle stages):**
- **v1 — flowering / unripe:** hanging vines bear small pale white
  closed bud-flowers + green immature pod-fruits clustered along the
  length of each vine
- **v2 — dormant:** vines still hang but bare — no flowers, no pods,
  still glowing faintly along their length
- **v3 — ripe harvest:** hanging vines bear glowing cyan-to-gold
  mature pods like living lanterns, no flowers
