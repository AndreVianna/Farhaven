# Violet Fleck (P00018) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the style anchor for the entire
Farhaven prop set. Keep art style, lighting, background, and composition
identical — only the silhouette and biology change per species.

## Species identity

Small alien wildflower with **pentagonal violet blooms marked by darker
violet speckles/flecks** across the petals. Each flower has 5 narrow
pointed petals arranged flat-radial around a small yellow-cream center.
Stems are branching — each individual stem from the ground carries 3-5
small flowers on slender sub-branches, giving an airy spray silhouette.
Multiple separate stems rise individually from the ground (NOT from a
shared bulb).

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (medium-violet petals with darker indigo-violet speckles
  dusting the petal surface, small creamy-yellow centers, branching
  wine-green stems, tiny round dark teal-green leaves)
- Flower shape (flat 5-pointed violet star / pentagon; 5 narrow pointed
  petals ~1.5 cm long each arranged radially around a small ~0.5 cm
  creamy-yellow center; individual flower ~3 cm across)
- Stem shape (branching wiry stem; one main stem from the ground,
  15-22 cm tall, splits into 2-3 slender sub-branches each carrying 1-2
  flowers; overall effect is an airy multi-flower spray)
- Leaf shape (tiny round teal-green leaves opposite on the stem nodes,
  each 0.5-1 cm across)
- Growth habit (**each main stem emerges individually from the ground** —
  not from a shared bulb; the patch is a scatter of 3-6 separate
  branching sprays over ~40 cm)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single patch, 3/4 high-angle, #888 gray background, soft
  studio lighting)

**VARIES (three reproductive cycle stages):**
- **v1 — mixed:** open violet flowers + some closed tight violet buds on
  the sub-branches, normal density
- **v2 — budding only:** fewer stems and fewer sub-branches, all flower
  positions bear closed tight violet buds, no open flowers
- **v3 — full bloom:** more stems and denser spray, every sub-branch
  bears an open pentagonal flower, no buds
