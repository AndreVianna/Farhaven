# Snow Bract (P00017) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the style anchor for the entire
Farhaven prop set. Keep art style, lighting, background, and composition
identical — only the silhouette and biology change per species.

## Species identity

Small alien wildflower where the showy "petals" are actually **cream-white
papery bracts** arranged star-like around a tiny central cluster of true
flowers. Similar to a poinsettia or edelweiss in concept — the bracts are
modified leaves, soft and matte-papery rather than glossy. Stems are short
and woody. Individual stems rise separately from the ground (NOT from a
shared bulb), scattered over a small patch.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (cream-white papery bracts with very faint gray-green
  veining on the underside showing through, tiny central flower cluster
  in warm pale-gold, short woody stems in deep olive-brown, small
  narrow teal-green true leaves lower on the stem)
- Flower shape (star-shape of 4 or 5 pointed papery bracts ~4 cm long
  each, arranged flat-radial around a tiny central cluster of 6-8
  minute true flowers ~0.5 cm total)
- Stem shape (short, stout, woody, 10-15 cm tall — the shortest stem
  of the flower set — with visible slight bark texture)
- Leaf shape (small narrow pointed teal-green true leaves alternating
  on the lower stem, 1-2 cm long)
- Growth habit (**each stem emerges individually from the ground** —
  not from a shared bulb; the patch is a scatter of 4-10 separate stems
  over ~35 cm)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single patch, 3/4 high-angle, #888 gray background, soft
  studio lighting)

**VARIES (three reproductive cycle stages):**
- **v1 — mixed:** open bract-stars + some closed green-cream pointed
  buds (bracts still folded up), normal density
- **v2 — budding only:** fewer stems, all tips bear closed green-cream
  pointed buds, no open bract-stars
- **v3 — full bloom:** more stems and denser patch, all tips bear fully
  opened bract-stars with visible central gold clusters, no buds
