# Silver Grass (P00009) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

The v1 `reference.png` of P00001 (Blade Grass) is the style anchor for the
entire Farhaven prop set. Keep the art style, lighting, background, and
general composition identical — only the silhouette and biology differ.

## What varies between variants / What stays fixed

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (silvery-blue, almost metallic — think brushed chrome
  catching a cool sky — with darker blue-steel shadow crevices and faint
  bioluminescent cyan edges)
- Material (very short flat blades; slightly waxy metallic sheen)
- Overall growth form (**short, spreading ground cover** — like a dense
  alien lawn, NOT a central tuft or fountain; blades all grow roughly
  upright from the soil they cover, no single base)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single patch subject, 3/4 high-angle, gray background, studio lighting)
- "Alien planet flora" feel

**VARIES (individual biological variation):**
- Blade length (very short young carpet vs slightly taller mature patch)
- Density (sparse newly-seeded vs dense established mat)
- Ground coverage (patchy vs full cover)
