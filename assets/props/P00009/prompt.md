# Silver Grass (P00009) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

The v1 `reference.png` of P00001 (Blade Grass) is the style anchor for the
entire Farhaven prop set. Keep the art style, lighting, background, and
general composition identical — only the silhouette and biology differ.

## What varies between variants / What stays fixed

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (silver-gray to pale platinum leaves, near-white feathery tufts at the tips, faint bioluminescent edges)
- Material (fine arching blades with airy feathery plumes — pampas-inspired)
- Overall growth form (fountain-like arching cluster; blades radiate outward and curve down, plumes rise above)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject, 3/4 high-angle, gray background, studio lighting)
- "Alien planet flora" feel

**VARIES (individual biological variation):**
- Presence and development of the feathery plumes (young buds vs open fluffy plumes)
- Blade density (sparse vs dense fountain)
- Overall size (compact vs wide spread)
