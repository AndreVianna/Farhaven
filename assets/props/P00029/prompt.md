# Forest Moss (P00029) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the style anchor. Keep art
style, lighting, background, and composition identical — only the

## Species identity

Deep dense **forest moss carpet** — the shade-loving ground cover of
the Forest biome. Distinct from the Grassland's Silver Grass and from
the Puff Sac: this is truly cushioned thick moss in deep greens with
strong bioluminescent vein networks. Reads as soft, wet, living.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (deep forest-green to near-black in shaded patches,
  cooler emerald-teal highlights where light catches; prominent
  cyan-to-mint bioluminescent vein networks glowing through the moss
  body, more vivid than in the Grassland equivalents; slightly damp
  surface highlights)
- Material (soft cushion-like moss with fine rounded pile texture,
  slightly translucent at the tips revealing the bioluminescent veins
  beneath)
- Overall growth form (**thick cushion carpet spreading horizontally
  on the ground, mounded in places up to 8 cm tall, with small
  fruiting spore capsules on short stalks rising up to 12 cm at a few
  points**)
- Scale (55-70 cm wide patch, 4-12 cm thick depending on mound)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte with subtle subsurface,
  painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single patch, 3/4 high-angle, gray background, soft
  studio lighting)

**VARIES (three density / maturity variants):**
- **v1 — fresh cushion:** thick even mat, strong glow veins, few spore stalks
- **v2 — young sparse:** patchy partial coverage, fainter glow,
  smaller mounds
- **v3 — mature mound:** tall cushioned mounds with many spore stalks,
  most vivid bioluminescence
