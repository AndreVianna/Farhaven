# Seed Pod Bush (P00003) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the **style anchor** for the
entire Farhaven prop set. Art style, lighting, background, and composition
must match. Only the silhouette/biology changes per species.

Species identity: knee-high woody bush that produces **fleshy oval seed pods**
through a clear reproductive cycle. Food source, flexible outer stems, radial
growth habit, hydration_level=4 (moderate), edibility_level=6 (good food).

## What varies between variants / What stays fixed

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (warm olive-brown woody stems, teal-green leaves)
- Material (woody flexible stems, leafy canopy, fleshy semi-translucent pods
  in ripe form)
- Overall growth form (radial multi-stem bush, upright branching, ~60 cm
  tall and ~70 cm wide)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte + subtle subsurface on pods,
  painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject, 3/4 high-angle, #888 gray background, soft
  even studio lighting with subtle cool rim light)

**VARIES (three reproductive cycle stages — NOT size/age variants):**
- **v1 — flowering / unripe:** yellow flowers + small green immature pods
- **v2 — dormant:** no flowers, no pods, leaves only
- **v3 — ripe harvest:** fleshy rose/coral/amber ripe pods, no flowers

Same individual bush, three phases of its annual cycle.
