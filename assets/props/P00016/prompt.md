# Crimson Cup (P00016) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the style anchor for the entire
Farhaven prop set. Keep art style, lighting, background, and composition
identical — only the silhouette and biology change per species.

## Species identity

Small alien wildflower with **deep-crimson tulip-cup blooms** held upright
on thick fleshy stems. Each flower is a closed-rim cup formed by six
broad fleshy petals — more substantial and waxy than daisy petals, more
compact than a bell. Multiple individual stems rise separately from the
ground (NOT from a shared bulb), scattered in a small patch.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (deep saturated crimson-red petals with very subtle
  darker vein lines, dark burgundy shadow inside the cup, thick stems
  in deep teal-green with a slightly waxy sheen, broad waxy teal-green
  leaves)
- Flower shape (upright tulip-style cup; 6 broad fleshy petals forming
  a rounded goblet about 5 cm tall and 4 cm across at the rim;
  petal tips meet or slightly overlap)
- Stem shape (thick fleshy straight stem, 18-25 cm tall, firm upright,
  noticeably stouter than a daisy stem)
- Leaf shape (broad waxy lanceolate teal-green leaves clasping the stem,
  2-3 per stem, 8-10 cm long)
- Growth habit (**each stem emerges individually from the ground** —
  not from a shared bulb; the patch is a scatter of 3-8 separate stems
  over ~35 cm)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single patch, 3/4 high-angle, #888 gray background, soft
  studio lighting)

**VARIES (three reproductive cycle stages):**
- **v1 — mixed:** open cups + some tightly closed teardrop buds, normal density
- **v2 — budding only:** fewer stems, all tips bear closed teardrop
  crimson buds, no open flowers
- **v3 — full bloom:** more stems and denser patch, all tips bear fully
  open cups, no buds
