# Earth Tongue (P00040) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the style anchor. Keep art
style, lighting, background, and composition identical — only the
silhouette and biology change per species.

## Species identity

A cluster of **tongue-shaped fungi** (inspired by Geoglossum /
Trichoglossum / jelly-baby) — small vertical fleshy blades poking up
from the ground like flat tongues. Dark and waxy, in small scattered
groups.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (dark slate-green fading to near-black at the tongue
  tips; waxy glossy surfaces with subtle amber-gold at the base where
  each tongue emerges from the ground; a very faint cyan
  bioluminescent midline running up the front face of each tongue)
- Material (flattened waxy tongue/blade shape, slightly sticky-looking
  surface, smooth rounded tips)
- Overall growth form (**a scattered group of individual vertical
  tongue-blades emerging from the ground at slightly different angles,
  each 5-12 cm tall, no cap no stipe, just a compressed vertical
  blade**)
- Scale (patch 20 cm wide, 8-15 blades per patch, tallest 12 cm)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single patch, 3/4 high-angle, gray background, soft
  studio lighting)

**VARIES (three density variants):**
- **v1 — sparse scattered:** 5-6 blades spread apart
- **v2 — cluster:** 10-12 blades in a tight group
- **v3 — dense carpet:** 20+ blades filling a wider patch, tallest
  reaching 14 cm
