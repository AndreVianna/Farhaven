## Species identity

A tall **rosette-shape fern** that grows as a crown of slender pinnate
fronds rising vertically from a compact low base, with the tips
arching outward. Unlike the squat Broad Frond Fern or the trunked
Tree Fern, this species has no visible trunk but is tall and upright
— like a giant shuttlecock of fronds.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (vivid medium-green fronds with slightly lighter
  lime-green young tips and darker forest-green mature interior
  fronds; a subtle golden-cream sheath visible at the base where
  fronds emerge from the crown center; faint cyan bioluminescent
  highlights at the frond tips)
- Material (finely pinnate painterly frond masses with slightly
  feathery silhouette edges)
- Overall growth form (**tall vertical rosette of pinnate fronds
  rising from a compact ground base, tops arching outward to form a
  shuttlecock crown**; no visible trunk)
- Scale (1.5-2 m tall, crown spread 1.2 m wide)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject framed vertically, 3/4 high-angle, gray
  background, soft studio lighting)

**VARIES (three growth-stage variants):**
- **v1 — mature baseline:** 1.8 m tall, 12 fronds in balanced crown
- **v2 — young:** 1.1 m tall, 6 fronds, tighter crown
- **v3 — old broad:** 2 m tall, 18 fronds, widest spread with a few
  inner fronds showing brown dry tips
