# Bracket Ring (P00041) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

Follow `assets/props/P00001/reference.png` as the style anchor. Keep art
style, lighting, background, and composition identical — only the
silhouette and biology change per species.

## Species identity

Bracket fungi growing in a **ring pattern around the trunk of a fallen
log** — multiple overlapping fan-shelves arranged as a near-complete
circle, giving the decomposing log a frilled crown. Complements
P00020 Shelf Fungus (which uses isolated clusters) with a more
dramatic ring-around-trunk composition.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (warm amber-brown upper surface of shelves grading to
  dark chocolate at the trunk-attached edge; pale cream pore layer
  underneath; darker rust-orange bands near each shelf rim; subtle
  cyan bioluminescent highlights on the outer edge of a few shelves)
- Material (fan-shaped bracket caps 10-15 cm wide, 1-2 cm thick at
  the trunk edge tapering to a thin outer rim; overlapping tiers)
- Overall growth form (**a ring of overlapping fan-bracket fungi
  encircling the mid-section of a fallen log segment, arranged like a
  decorative collar around the trunk**)
- Scale (log segment 90 cm long, 30 cm thick; ring of fungi occupies
  ~25 cm along the log; 10-18 brackets per ring depending on variant)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single log + ring-fungi, 3/4 high-angle, gray background,
  soft studio lighting)

**VARIES (three host-log variants matching fallen trees of the Forest):**
- **v1 — Canopy Giant host trunk:** warm gray-brown bark with smooth
  vertical striations (matches P00021)
- **v2 — Ribbon Bark Tree host:** dark charcoal bark with visible
  peeling rust-orange patches (matches P00023)
- **v3 — Glow Vine Tree host:** warm gray-brown bark + faint cyan
  bioluminescent seepage still glowing in bark cracks (matches P00024)
