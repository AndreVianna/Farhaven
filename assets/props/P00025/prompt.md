## Species identity

A Forest canopy tree whose signature is **large clustered bioluminescent
seed pods** hanging in grape-like bunches from the lower canopy. Pods
are the harvestable reproductive structure.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (warm gray-brown trunk, dark teal-green canopy with
  cooler blue-green shadow tints; ripe pods in warm amber-to-cyan
  gradient with clear bioluminescent glow; unripe pods matte jade-green)
- Material (rigid woody trunk, painterly canopy foliage, pods as
  chubby rounded fruit-shapes)
- Overall growth form (**moderate trunk + rounded canopy + hanging
  grape-like clusters of large pods from lower branches**)
- Scale (4.5 m tall, trunk 28 cm thick, canopy 3 m across, pod
  clusters 50 cm across each)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject framed vertically, 3/4 high-angle, gray
  background, soft studio lighting)

**VARIES (three reproductive cycle stages):**
- **v1 — flowering / unripe:** small white flowers + matte green
  unripe pod clusters
- **v2 — dormant:** bare branches, no flowers, no pods — just trunk +
  canopy
- **v3 — ripe harvest:** large amber-to-cyan glowing pod clusters,
  no flowers
