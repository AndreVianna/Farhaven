## Species identity

A Forest tree bearing **large heavy pendulous fruits** that droop from
the branches like alien jackfruit/durian. The fruits are massive and
visually dominant, making the tree bow under their weight when ripe.

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (warm gray-tan trunk, teal-green canopy with cooler
  shadow tints; ripe fruits in deep amber-maroon with scattered
  pale-cream lenticels dotting the rind; unripe fruits smaller and
  green)
- Material (rigid woody trunk, painterly canopy foliage, fruits as
  large ovoid shapes with textured rough skin)
- Overall growth form (**broad canopy with heavy pendulous fruits
  hanging from branches, branches visibly sagging under weight**)
- Scale (4 m tall, trunk 30 cm thick, canopy 3.5 m across, ripe fruits
  25–30 cm long each)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject framed vertically, 3/4 high-angle, gray
  background, soft studio lighting)

**VARIES (three reproductive cycle stages):**
- **v1 — flowering / unripe:** magenta flowers + small green immature
  fruits at base of flowers
- **v2 — dormant:** bare canopy, no flowers, no fruits
- **v3 — ripe harvest:** heavy amber-maroon fruits bowing branches
  downward, no flowers
