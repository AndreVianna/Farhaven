# Fan Fern (P00011) — Generation Prompts

## Style Anchor

The v1 `reference.png` of P00001 (Blade Grass) is the style anchor for the
entire Farhaven prop set. Keep the art style, lighting, background, and
general composition identical — only the silhouette and biology differ.

## What varies between variants / What stays fixed

**FIXED (species identity):**
- Color palette (medium teal-green fronds with darker blue-green veins
  and deeper shadow crevices, very faint cyan bioluminescent edges on
  the frond margins; short cream-colored stems at the base)
- Material (broad flat fan-shaped fronds with visible radiating ribs,
  slightly waxy matte surface — NOT spiraled, NOT feathery, NOT
  narrow-leaved)
- Overall growth form (**horizontal fan-shaped fronds spreading outward
  from a low compact base** — each frond is a flat semicircular fan,
  like a small alien palmetto or ginkgo-style leaf, radiating ribs
  visible; multiple fronds overlap at slight angles to form a low
  spreading canopy close to the ground)
- Scale (small ground plant, ~30 cm tall with a spread of ~45 cm wide)
- Art style (Subnautica/NMS, stylized, matte, painterly, not photorealistic)
- Composition (single subject, 3/4 high-angle, gray background, studio lighting)
- "Alien planet flora" feel

**VARIES (individual biological variation):**
- Number of fronds (sparse 3-4 vs full 5-7 vs dense 8+)
- Frond size (smaller young vs full-sized mature)
- Overlap pattern (tidy symmetric vs irregular splay)

## Variation 1 (Baseline — full mature, 5-6 fronds) — `reference_v1.png` → `mesh_v1.glb`

```
A single alien fan-fern plant. Low compact base with five to six flat
fan-shaped fronds radiating outward in a spreading canopy. Each frond

edge — similar in shape to a small alien palmetto or ginkgo blade.

horizontal spread ~45 cm wide and ~30 cm tall at the highest frond.

cyan bioluminescent edges on the frond margins. Short cream-colored
stems connect the fronds to the compact ground base.

Art style: Stylized 3D game asset in the visual language of Subnautica
and No Man's Sky — clean organic shapes, slightly exaggerated silhouette
for readability, matte shading, alien planet flora with an otherworldly
feel. Semi-realistic but painterly. Not photorealistic. Single pop of
cool teal against muted palette. Clean readable silhouette reading
unmistakably as a fan-shaped fern, NOT spiraled.

Composition: Single subject centered, 3/4 high-angle view, isolated on
```
