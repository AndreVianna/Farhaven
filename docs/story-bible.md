# Farhaven — Story Bible

*Living document. Updated as the narrative evolves.*

---

## Premise

A colonial convoy of ships is en route to establish a new human settlement in a distant star system. Colonists travel in suspended animation inside escape-capable hibernation pods. The player — a military-trained colonist — is unknowingly reassigned to the convoy's military escort vessel, separated from their family.

The military ship is attacked. Pods are ejected. The player crash-lands on an unknown planet.

---

## The Planet

- **Not the intended destination.** The player initially believes it matches the colony photos, but it's a different world.
- **Previously inhabited.** An alien civilization lived here — roughly equivalent to Earth's 20th century (early space exploration, industrial infrastructure, global communications).
- **Extinct.** Destroyed by global nuclear war tens of thousands of years ago. Radiation has long since dissipated. Nature has fully reclaimed the surface.
- **What remains:** Fragmented concrete, rusted metal, buried bunkers, collapsed structures — all overgrown. Nothing functional, but enough to scavenge components.
- **Biomes:** Crash Site, Grassland, Forest, Rocky, Water. The planet is hospitable — breathable atmosphere, edible flora (some toxic), fauna (some hostile at night).

---

## The Convoy

- **Colonial program:** Organized civilian colonization of a new star system. Families sign up, receive training, and ship out.
- **Structure:** Multiple ships — civilian colony ships + military escort vessel.
- **The attack:** Only the military ship was targeted. The rest of the convoy was captured (not destroyed).
- **The family:** The player's family is on a civilian ship. They are alive — captured, not killed.
- **The conspiracy:** The player was reassigned to the military ship without their knowledge. Why? By whom? This is a central mystery.

---

## The Player

- **Background:** Military training (shown by uniform in opening cutscene). Colonist by choice — signed up with family for a new life.
- **Starting state:** Alone, no memory of the attack (was in hibernation), confused about location.
- **Motivation arc:**
  1. Survive (immediate)
  2. Understand where they are (Chapter 1-2)
  3. Find out what happened (Chapter 2-3)
  4. Rescue family and colonists (Chapter 3-4+)
  5. Complete the original journey (ending)

## Anomalies (Two Types)

### Human Anomalies 🧑
- **Crashed escape pods** from the military ship
- Each pod tells a story: personal logs, flight data, last moments
- Some occupants dead, some TBD
- Provide pieces of the larger puzzle (what happened during the attack)

### Alien Anomalies 🔶
- **Ruins of the extinct civilization**
- Concrete, metal, bunker entrances, antenna fragments, collapsed structures
- Overgrown — plants growing through cracks, buried under soil/vegetation
- Not high-tech or exotic — mundane industrial/military infrastructure, just alien
- Scannable for Journal entries that piece together the civilization's history

### The First Anomaly (Chapter 1 Climax)
Both types in one location: a crashed human pod that impacted directly onto an exposed alien concrete structure. The pod occupant died on impact — killed by the collision with the buried ruin. The alien structure cracked open on impact, revealing its interior for the first time.

This creates the dual cliffhanger:
1. "There are other military survivors (or were). How many? Where?"
2. "What IS this thing under the ground? Who built it?"

## Chapter Structure

### Chapter 1 — Landfall (Free)
**Theme:** Survival and discovery
**Arc:** Wake up → survive → build base → find the anomaly

- **Opening cutscene:** Family signs up → boarding → hibernation → attack chaos → crash → awakening
- **Gameplay progression:**
  - Days 1-2: Basic survival — gather, eat, drink, explore immediate area
  - Days 3-4: First nights with hostile fauna → need for shelter becomes urgent
  - Days 5+: Shelter built → workbench inside → tool upgrades → access to blocked area
- **Climax:** Reach anomaly location (gated behind tool upgrade chain: shelter → workbench → upgrade). Discover crashed pod + alien structure.
- **Cliffhanger:** Two Journal entries. Two questions. Chapter complete.

### Chapter 2 — Echoes (~$2.50)
**Theme:** Exploration and revelation
**Rough direction:**
- Explore wider map. More ruins of the civilization.
- Discover this is NOT the colony planet.
- Find more pods / possible survivor(s)?
- Another crash site?
- Piece together what happened to the convoy.

### Chapter 3 — Ascent (~$2.50)
**Theme:** Escape and truth
**Rough direction:**
- Reach the partially destroyed military ship in orbit
- Use scavenged alien tech + human tech to get there
- Access ship logs — learn about the reassignment, the attack, the capture
- Discover family is alive

### Chapter 4+ — Rescue (~$2.50 each)
**Theme:** The original mission, transformed
**Rough direction:**
- Go after the captured convoy
- Use what was learned on the planet
- Rescue family and colonists
- Arrive at the colony destination — completing the journey that started it all

## Tone & Themes

- **Curiosity over punishment.** The planet is beautiful and mysterious, not hostile and grim.
- **Warm visual identity.** Astroneer meets MLU — low-poly, colorful, charming.
- **Story drives retention.** The mysteries pull the player forward. Crafting/building serve the narrative.
- **Mirror of humanity.** The extinct civilization made the same mistakes we almost made. Nuclear war ended them. The player walks through the aftermath — a cautionary echo.
- **Family as anchor.** Everything connects back to the family. The survival isn't abstract — it's "I need to get back to them."
- **Hope, not despair.** The ending is arrival, reunion, completion. Not tragedy.
